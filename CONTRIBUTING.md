# Contributing guide

Entropic Science values all forms of contribution: code, research, writing, organizing, promoting, funding, and reviewing. You do not need to be a programmer to contribute meaningfully.

Please read the [Code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) before participating.

## Getting started

**Step 1: Join Discord** at [discord.gg/2EbveaB2wS](https://discord.gg/2EbveaB2wS) and introduce yourself in `#introductions`. Mention your background, interests, and what kind of work you would like to do.

**Step 2: Explore active projects.** Browse the repositories listed in the [README](README.md) and the task boards on [GitHub Projects](https://github.com/orgs/Entropic-Science/projects). Look for issues labeled `good-first-issue` for accessible starting points.

**Step 3: Start contributing.** Pick any of the activities listed below. No formal approval is needed to begin. If your work involves code, submit a clearly articulated pull request to the relevant repository. If your work is non-code, share it in the `#contributions` channel on Discord.

## Divisions

Work is organized into three divisions, roughly in order of current priority. Members and projects are listed in the [knowledge base](https://github.com/Entropic-Science/knowledge-base). To join a division, add a row to [MEMBERS.md](https://github.com/Entropic-Science/knowledge-base/blob/main/MEMBERS.md). To propose a project, add a row to [PROJECTS.md](https://github.com/Entropic-Science/knowledge-base/blob/main/PROJECTS.md).

### Infrastructure
Software development on quantum-random AI/LLM inference pipelines, QRNG hardware integration, gRPC server standards, PRNG/QRNG comparison modes, evaluation harnesses, and alternative entropy sources (DRAM timing noise, etc.).

### Research
Designing and running rigorous experiments comparing QRNG-enhanced and deterministic AI systems: experimental design, data collection, analysis, and reporting. Developing and refining conceptual frameworks connecting quantum mechanics, consciousness, and AI alignment: literature synthesis, philosophical analysis, and formal modeling. Research papers and preprints.

### Outreach-Fundraising
Grant applications, partnership development, investor outreach, and helping connect people with projects and projects with resources. Blog posts, articles, social media content, podcast appearances, conference talks, tutorials, and guides. Onboarding new members, moderation, organizing events, mentoring, and recruiting.

Review and QA (code review, peer review of research, testing, feedback on proposals and documents) is welcome across all divisions.

## How to contribute code

1. Fork the relevant repository
2. Create a branch with a descriptive name
3. Make your changes with clear commit messages
4. Open a pull request. In the PR description, explain what the change does and why. Link any related GitHub issues.
5. A maintainer will review your PR. Address feedback and iterate.

For write access to repositories or project boards, ask an admin on Discord.

## Research co-authorship

Anyone who contributes meaningfully to a research paper has the right to be included as an author. Each paper has a lead author – normally the person driving the work – who proposes the author list and order. If the ordering is contested, the admins investigate and make a final decision.
